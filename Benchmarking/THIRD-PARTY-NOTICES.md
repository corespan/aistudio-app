## Why this matters here

This app is publicly deployed, so serving the bundle **is** distribution.
MIT, BSD and ISC all condition redistribution on carrying the copyright
notice; Apache-2.0 section 4(d) requires reproducing any NOTICE file;
OFL-1.1 section 2 requires the notice to accompany the fonts. 0BSD is the
one exception — it drops the retention clause deliberately — and is
reproduced anyway.

Vite minifies with esbuild, which strips comments, so none of that
survives into the bundle on its own. The full texts are therefore emitted
to `public/third-party-licences.txt`, which Vite copies into `dist/` and
the app links from its footer — so the notices travel with the thing
being distributed rather than sitting in a repository the visitor never
opens.

A few entries are types-only (`@types/react`, `csstype`) or reachable only
on paths this app does not use, and contribute no runtime code to the
bundle. They are listed regardless: attributing more than strictly
required is free, and pruning the list by hand is how inventories start
disagreeing with what actually ships.

devDependencies are excluded. Build tooling is never conveyed to anyone,
so no notice obligation attaches to it.
